# Roti (Indian Flatbread)

https://biggerbolderbaking.com/roti-chapati-recipe

Also known as chapati. A simple whole wheat flatbread that pairs well with curries, dal, and just about anything.

## Ingredients
* 1 cup (142g) atta flour or white whole wheat flour
* 1/4 tsp salt
* 4 tsp vegetable oil
* 1/3 to 1/2 cup warm water
* 1/4 cup (57g) butter or ghee, melted (for brushing)
* Extra flour for dusting

## Substitution
If you can't find atta flour, use a 50/50 blend of regular whole wheat flour and all-purpose flour.

## Instructions
1. Mix flour and salt in a bowl. Add oil and rub into the flour until combined
2. Gradually add water and knead to form a dough, starting with 1/3 cup and adding more as needed
3. Knead for about 5 minutes until smooth and pliable (should feel like the softness of your earlobe)
4. Cover and let rest for at least 15-30 minutes
5. Divide dough into 8 equal portions. Roll each into a smooth ball and flatten slightly
6. Dust with flour and roll each ball into a thin 6" round, dusting as needed to prevent sticking
7. Heat a dry cast-iron skillet or tawa over medium heat until very hot
8. Place roti on the skillet and cook about 1 minute until bubbles form on the surface
9. Flip with tongs and cook the other side for about 30 seconds
10. Using tongs, place the roti directly on the open flame until it puffs up, just a few seconds per side. Move it around so it doesn't burn in one spot
11. Brush immediately with ghee or melted butter
12. Stack cooked rotis and keep wrapped in a clean towel to stay warm and soft

## Notes
- If you don't have a gas burner, press down lightly on the roti with a spatula while it's on the skillet to encourage puffing
- The dough can rest for up to 3 hours; longer rest = easier to roll
- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 2 months
- Reheat on a dry skillet over low heat or in the microwave

Makes 8 rotis
