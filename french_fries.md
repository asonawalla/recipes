# French Fries

Adapted from The Cozy Cook: https://thecozycook.com/homemade-french-fries/

## Ingredients

- 10 large russet potatoes
- 2 quarts vegetable or canola oil
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Peel and cut potatoes into 1/2-inch sticks.
2. Soak in cold water for at least 1 hour and up to overnight to remove starch.
3. Rinse twice, then dry very thoroughly with towels.
4. Heat oil to 300 F.
5. Fry in batches for 5 to 6 minutes. This first fry cooks the potatoes without really browning them.
6. Transfer to paper towels while you finish the rest.
7. Raise the oil temperature to 400 F.
8. Fry each batch again for about 5 minutes, until crisp and golden brown.
9. Drain on fresh paper towels and salt immediately.

## Notes

- Russets work best here.
- Do not crowd the pot or the fries will be less crisp.
- If baking instead: soak and dry as above, toss with oil, bake at 400 F for 15 to 20 minutes, flip, then bake another 10 minutes or until browned and crisp.
