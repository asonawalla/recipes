# Tangzhong

Adapted from King Arthur Baking: https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2018/07/23/how-to-convert-a-bread-recipe-to-tangzhong

## Ingredients

### Standard Tangzhong Ratio
- 1 part flour
- 5 parts liquid

### Common Shortcut
- 23 g flour
- 113 to 115 g liquid

## Instructions

1. Use about 5 to 10% of the total flour in a bread recipe for the tangzhong.
2. Take that flour and 5 times its weight in liquid from the recipe itself, not as extra ingredients.
3. Cook the flour and liquid together over medium heat, stirring, until it thickens into a smooth paste.
4. Let it cool briefly, then add it with the rest of the dough ingredients.
5. When converting a soft bread recipe, aim for about 75% overall hydration so the dough does not end up too stiff.

## Notes

- This method works best for soft breads like sandwich loaves, dinner rolls, and buns.
- A simple starting point for recipes with about 3 to 4 cups of flour is 23 g flour plus 113 to 115 g liquid for the tangzhong.
- The flour and liquid used for tangzhong come out of the recipe totals.
- Tangzhong usually gives a softer crumb and helps bread stay fresh longer.
