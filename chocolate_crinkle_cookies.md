# Chocolate Crinkle Cookies

Adapted from Sally's Baking Addiction: https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/double-chocolate-crinkle-cookies/

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup + 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/8 tsp salt
- 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips

### For Rolling
- 3 tbsp granulated sugar
- 1 cup powdered sugar

## Instructions

1. Beat butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until light and fluffy.
2. Beat in egg and vanilla until fully combined.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt.
4. Add dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until combined, then fold in the chocolate chips.
5. Cover and chill the dough for at least 3 hours. The dough will be very sticky before chilling.
6. Preheat oven to 350 F and line baking sheets with parchment.
7. Scoop dough into balls, about 1.5 tablespoons each.
8. Roll each dough ball lightly in granulated sugar, then heavily in powdered sugar.
9. Place on the baking sheet a few inches apart and bake for 11 to 12 minutes, until the edges look set and the centers are still soft.
10. Cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack to cool completely.

## Notes

- Chilling is important here for both flavor and texture, and it makes the dough possible to handle.
- The granulated sugar under the powdered sugar helps keep the white coating from melting away as much in the oven.
- If the cookies are not spreading by about 9 minutes, tap the baking sheet on the counter a couple of times, then finish baking.
