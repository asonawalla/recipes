# Chicken Shawarma

Adapted from RecipeTin Eats: https://www.recipetineats.com/chicken-sharwama-middle-eastern/

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs

### Marinade
- 1 large garlic clove, minced
- 1 tbsp ground coriander
- 1 tbsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp ground cardamom
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp salt
- Black pepper
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 3 tbsp olive oil

### To Serve
- Flatbreads or pita
- Yogurt sauce
- Lettuce
- Tomato
- Red onion, thinly sliced
- Hot sauce (optional)
- Shredded cheese (optional)

## Instructions

1. Combine all marinade ingredients in a bowl or zip-top bag.
2. Add chicken and coat well. Marinate for at least 3 hours, ideally overnight.
3. Heat a large skillet or grill over medium-high heat and lightly oil if needed.
4. Cook chicken for 4 to 5 minutes on the first side until well browned and lightly charred.
5. Flip and cook another 3 to 4 minutes, until cooked through.
6. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes, then slice if desired.
7. Serve in flatbread with yogurt sauce, lettuce, tomato, onion, and any other toppings.

## Notes

- Chicken thighs work best here, but chicken breast also works if pounded or sliced thinner and cooked a little faster.
- Stove or grill gives the best char, but you can also bake at 425 F for 20 to 25 minutes.
- This marinates well ahead of time and can be frozen in the marinade.
