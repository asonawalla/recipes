# Focaccia

Adapted from this video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qALTVKlyhwI

## Ingredients

- 500 g strong flour
- 4 to 6 g active dry yeast
- 1 tsp maple syrup, sugar, or malt
- 380 to 400 g lukewarm water
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tsp salt

### For the Pan / Top
- Extra virgin olive oil
- A little water
- Coarse salt

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine flour, yeast, sweetener, and 350 g of the water.
2. Mix, then add more water as needed until the dough is very soft and sticky. Around 380 g total water is the target in the source recipe.
3. Add olive oil and salt, then mix until the dough looks smoother and has absorbed most of the oil.
4. Cover and rest 30 minutes.
5. Fold the dough in the bowl a few times to build strength, then cover and rest 1 hour.
6. Fold once more with wet or oiled hands.
7. Oil a baking tray generously, transfer the dough to it, fold it over itself once or twice, and coat the top lightly with oil.
8. Cover the tray and let rise 30 to 45 minutes while preheating the oven as hot as it will go, ideally around 525 to 530 F, with the rack in the lowest position.
9. Oil your hands and press the dough from the center toward the edges until it fills the tray. Dimple firmly with your fingers.
10. Top with a little more oil, a splash of water, and coarse salt.
11. Bake 15 to 20 minutes, until deeply golden with a crisp crust.
12. Cool on a wire rack for 10 to 15 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

- The source uses a 25 x 35 cm tray, about 10 x 14 inches.
- A strong flour around 12% protein is recommended.
- If your oven runs hot, back the temperature off a bit and watch closely.
