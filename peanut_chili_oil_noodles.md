# Peanut-Chili Oil Noodles with Crispy Shredded Tofu

Adapted from Vegan Scratch Kitchen: https://veganscratchkitchen.substack.com/p/peanut-chili-oil-noodles-w-crispy

## Ingredients

### Crispy Shredded Tofu

- 1 block super-firm tofu, drained, patted dry, and shredded with a box grater
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 4 to 5 pinches kosher salt
- 3 tablespoons avocado oil

### Peanut-Chili Oil Noodles

- A-Sha noodles or another ramen-style noodle, cooked (the source does not specify an amount)
- 2/3 cup creamy peanut butter
- 1 small shallot, minced
- 1-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled and minced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons chili crisp
- 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1/3 cup avocado oil
- Reserved noodle cooking water, as needed

### Garnish

- Thinly sliced green onions
- Sesame seeds
- Chili oil

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 425 F and line a sheet pan with parchment paper.
2. Add the shredded tofu to the pan. Season with the garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, and kosher salt. Add 3 tablespoons avocado oil and toss to coat, then spread the tofu evenly across the pan.
3. Roast for 30 minutes, turning the tofu and shaking the pan halfway through.
4. Meanwhile, cook the noodles according to the package instructions. Reserve some cooking water, drain the noodles, rinse them under cold water to prevent sticking, and set aside.
5. When the tofu is nearly done, combine the peanut butter, shallot, ginger, garlic, green onions, chili crisp, sesame seeds, soy sauce, and rice vinegar in a heat-safe stainless-steel saute pan.
6. Heat 1/3 cup avocado oil in a separate medium pot over medium-high heat until it just begins to smoke. Carefully pour the hot oil over the sauce ingredients and whisk until combined.
7. Add the noodles to the sauce and toss until evenly coated. If the sauce is too thick, loosen it with a little reserved noodle cooking water.
8. Divide the noodles among bowls and top with the crispy shredded tofu. Garnish with green onions, sesame seeds, and chili oil.

## Notes

- The source describes this as an approximately 30-minute meal but does not specify the noodle quantity or number of servings.
