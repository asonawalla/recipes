Halal Guys-Style White Sauce
============================

Creamy, tangy, and herby — Kewpie mayo makes this extra rich and silky

Servings: 4

Ingredients

- 0.5 cups Kewpie mayo
- 1 tablespoons plain yogurt
- 1 teaspoons white vinegar
- 1 teaspoons lemon juice, fresh or bottled
- 0.5 teaspoons sugar
- 0.5 teaspoons dried oregano
- 0.3 teaspoons garlic powder
- 0.3 teaspoons onion powder
- 0.3 teaspoons black pepper
- 0.3 teaspoons salt
